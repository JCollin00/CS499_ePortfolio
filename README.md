## About This Portfolio

Welcome to my Computer Science Capstone ePortfolio.

This portfolio represents the culmination of my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University. It demonstrates the knowledge, technical skills, problem-solving abilities, and professional development I have gained throughout my academic journey.

I began my Computer Science studies in 2021 at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) and continued my academic journey at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout my studies, I developed experience in software development, software engineering, algorithms and data structures, databases, programming, and computer science practices.

The purpose of this portfolio is to provide evidence of my technical abilities through original academic work and enhanced versions of selected artifacts.
