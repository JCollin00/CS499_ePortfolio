Professional Self-Assessment

A reflection on my academic journey, technical development, professional goals, and Computer Science program outcomes.

Introduction

Completing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science represents an important milestone in my academic and professional development. I began my Computer Science studies in 2021 at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) and continued my academic journey at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout this journey, I have developed a strong foundation in programming, software engineering, algorithms and data structures, databases, and computer science problem solving.

My experience in the Computer Science program has taught me that professional software development involves much more than writing code. A successful software solution requires understanding the problem, analyzing requirements, selecting appropriate technologies, designing maintainable systems, testing solutions, protecting data, and communicating technical decisions clearly. The three enhanced artifacts included in this portfolio demonstrate this progression from simply completing software projects to evaluating and improving them as a developing software professional.

Development Throughout the Computer Science Program

One of the most important areas of growth during my studies has been my understanding of software design. Object-oriented programming helped me learn how to organize software into reusable and maintainable components. My software engineering coursework further expanded this knowledge by introducing software development lifecycles, requirements analysis, modular design, testing, documentation, version control, and software maintenance.

I also developed a stronger understanding of algorithms and data structures. These concepts taught me that a program should not only produce the correct result, but should also use computational resources effectively. Understanding algorithmic complexity, including Big-O notation, helped me evaluate different approaches and recognize the importance of performance, scalability, and efficiency when designing software.

Database development provided another important area of professional growth. I learned how software applications depend on reliable data storage, retrieval, validation, and management. Integrating applications with databases helped me understand the relationship between application logic and persistent data and reinforced the importance of data integrity and security.

Computer Science Program Outcome 1

Collaborative Environments and Organizational Decision Making

The first program outcome focuses on employing strategies for building collaborative environments that enable diverse audiences to support organizational decision making in computer science. Throughout my program, I developed this ability through project planning, documentation, code reviews, version control, and communication of technical decisions.

My experience with Git and GitHub helped me understand the importance of collaborative software development. Version control provides a structured way to manage changes, preserve previous versions, and communicate development progress. Documentation and code comments also help other developers understand the purpose and behavior of a system.

My ePortfolio itself demonstrates this outcome because I organized my work so that another developer, instructor, or potential employer can easily understand the purpose of each artifact and the changes made during the enhancement process.

Computer Science Program Outcome 2

Professional Communication

The second outcome focuses on designing, developing, and delivering professional-quality oral, written, and visual communication. Communication became an increasingly important part of my studies because technical knowledge has limited value if I cannot explain my decisions clearly to others.

The narratives associated with my three portfolio categories required me to explain the purpose of each artifact, identify weaknesses in the original implementation, describe the enhancements I made, and connect those improvements to specific Computer Science skills. The code review also required me to communicate technical information verbally and demonstrate that I could explain software decisions rather than simply implement them.

Building this GitHub Pages portfolio further strengthened my ability to present technical information to a professional audience. I organized the portfolio with clear navigation, descriptive sections, consistent naming, and accessible content so that visitors can quickly locate the information they need.

Computer Science Program Outcome 3

Algorithmic Problem Solving

The third outcome focuses on designing and evaluating computing solutions using algorithmic principles and computer science practices while considering trade-offs in design decisions.

My Algorithms and Data Structures enhancement demonstrates this outcome by requiring me to evaluate the original implementation and identify opportunities to improve its efficiency. I learned that selecting an algorithm requires more than considering whether it produces the correct result. Factors such as time complexity, space requirements, scalability, and the characteristics of the input data must also be considered.

The use of Big-O analysis provides a way to communicate these performance characteristics objectively. This experience improved my ability to evaluate multiple solutions and select an approach based on measurable technical considerations rather than simply choosing the first solution that works.

Computer Science Program Outcome 4

Computing Techniques, Skills, and Tools

The fourth outcome focuses on using well-founded and innovative techniques, skills, and tools to implement computing solutions that deliver value and accomplish industry-specific goals.

My Computer Science program provided experience with multiple programming languages, development tools, databases, version control systems, and software development practices. Working with languages such as C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL, HTML, and CSS allowed me to understand that different technologies are appropriate for different problems.

My portfolio enhancements allowed me to apply these skills rather than simply study them theoretically. I evaluated existing implementations, identified areas for improvement, modified code, tested changes, documented decisions, and organized the resulting work for professional presentation.

This process helped me develop a more complete software development mindset. Instead of viewing programming as an isolated activity, I now understand it as part of a larger process involving analysis, design, implementation, testing, documentation, maintenance, and communication.

Computer Science Program Outcome 5

Security Mindset

The fifth outcome focuses on developing a security mindset that anticipates vulnerabilities, mitigates design flaws, and protects privacy and data resources.

Security has become an important consideration throughout my development process. I learned that security should not be treated as a final step added after an application has already been designed. Instead, security should be considered during requirements analysis, system design, implementation, testing, and deployment.

Practices such as input validation, appropriate error handling, protecting sensitive information, authentication, access control, and careful management of application data can reduce security risks. My database and software engineering work reinforced the importance of protecting data integrity and considering potential vulnerabilities when designing software.

Developing this security mindset will be particularly valuable in my professional career because modern applications frequently process sensitive information and operate in environments where security threats are constantly evolving.

Career Goals

My long-term career goal is to become a professional software developer capable of designing, developing, and maintaining reliable software solutions. I am particularly interested in continuing to develop my knowledge of application development, software architecture, databases, and modern software engineering practices.

The skills represented in this portfolio provide a foundation for that career. I can analyze problems, develop software solutions, evaluate algorithms, work with databases, use development tools, document technical decisions, and consider security throughout the development process.

I also recognize that completing a Computer Science degree is not the end of my learning. Technology changes continuously, and successful software professionals must continue learning new frameworks, programming languages, development methodologies, security practices, and emerging technologies. I intend to continue developing these skills throughout my career.

Conclusion

This ePortfolio represents more than a collection of academic assignments. It provides evidence of my development as a Computer Science student and as an emerging software professional. The enhancement process required me to evaluate previous work, identify weaknesses, implement improvements, and explain the technical decisions behind those improvements.

Through this process, I have developed greater confidence in my ability to approach software development systematically. I understand the importance of software quality, algorithmic efficiency, database reliability, professional communication, collaboration, and security.

As I move from academic study into the professional field, I will continue building on these skills. My goal is not only to become a capable programmer, but to become a responsible software professional who can analyze complex problems, develop practical solutions, communicate effectively, and continuously improve both my technical abilities and the systems I create.
